Roadmap

Build the evidence chain in order.

The agent is not phase one. Watchpost first needs reliable collection, durable state, deterministic rules, and coherent incidents—the substrate an operational agent must be able to trust.

0 · Contracts and threat model

Vocabulary, trust zones, state machines, signal quality, clock semantics, action boundaries, and representative fixtures.

1 · Single-node foundation

Go service, embedded Nift frontend, SQLite migrations, authentication, capabilities, audit, diagnostics, and portable releases.

2 · Inventory and collection

Targets, collectors, host metrics, endpoint checks, typed observations, retention, freshness, cardinality, and backpressure.

3 · Rules, alerts, and notifications

Deterministic evaluation, duration, hysteresis, missing-data policy, maintenance, deduplication, email, and webhooks.

4 · Incidents and operational UX

Durable timelines, correlation, dashboards, charts, logs, saved views, assignment, and resolution.

5 · Evidence-grounded agent

Provider-independent investigation, visible tools, citations, durable conversations, redaction, and adversarial evaluation.

6 · Typed actions and approvals

Action registry, dry runs, policy, approval, execution, verification, rollback, replay defence, and immutable audit.

7 · Fleet and federation

Node identity, offline operation, selective sharing, store-and-forward, tenancy, revocation, and reconciliation.

8 · Hardening and first release

Long-run resource campaigns, fuzzing, disaster recovery, security review, installers, upgrade rehearsal, and dogfooding.
