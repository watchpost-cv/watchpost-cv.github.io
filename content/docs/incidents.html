Incidents

An alert says a condition is active. An incident preserves the operational episode: what happened, what evidence was considered, who decided what, and how the system recovered.

Durable timeline

An incident timeline may contain alert transitions, selected signal windows, target and topology changes, deployments, log evidence, operator notes, agent tool results, approvals, actions, and post-action verification.

Correlation without concealment

Watchpost may suggest that alerts share a cause or belong to one incident. Suggestions must remain visible and reversible. Unrelated alert histories are not silently merged merely because their timestamps overlap.

Human state

Incidents need severity, status, owner, acknowledgement, assignment, notes, and a resolution summary. These are first-class durable records with actor identity and time—not mutable annotations attached only to a notification.

Agent conversations

Investigation conversations attach to an incident or target and survive browser closure and server restart. The incident retains useful evidence and conclusions without requiring operators to reconstruct a chat transcript from memory.

Resolution

Resolution records what stopped the incident, what evidence supports recovery, and whether follow-up remains. Closing an incident does not delete its alerts or history.
