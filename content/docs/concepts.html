Core concepts

Watchpost uses a deliberately small vocabulary. Keeping these objects distinct prevents monitoring from becoming an opaque pile of timestamped JSON.

Watchpost and fleet

A Watchpost is one independently useful deployed node. A fleet connects nodes for selective coordination without making them dependent on continuous upstream connectivity.

Post, post kind, and collector

A post is anything monitored: a host, service, endpoint, application, database, network device, power device, or sensor. This is the canonical term throughout the UI, API, schema, and documentation. A post kind describes its typed capabilities and expected observations. A collector obtains observations from a post. Posts carry stable identity, ownership, labels, topology, and maintenance state; collectors carry version, authentication, limits, and health.

Signal and event

A signal is a timestamped measurement or state observation. An event is a meaningful occurrence or transition. Both retain source and time; signals additionally need quality, freshness, and units where applicable.

missing, stale, unknown, and bad quality are operational states. None is equivalent to numeric zero.

Rule, alert, and incident

A rule deterministically evaluates evidence. An alert is the active lifecycle produced by that rule. An incident is the larger durable episode that may group several alerts with evidence, notes, ownership, investigation, actions, and resolution.

Action

An action is a typed operation available under explicit policy. Investigation does not imply action authority. Recommendations, approvals, executions, and verification are separate records.
